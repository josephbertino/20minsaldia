    Push, Pull and Pierna split the total time evenly across the 5 exercises, with a short break between them. Reto 20 and Cindy are one countdown for the whole time. When Reto 20 includes a timed hold, a hold button appears under the round counter. Tap it each round: you get 5 s to get in position (three ticks and the “go” beep), then the hold counts down, with three ticks and the horn when it ends. While a hold runs, the big clock shows the hold and the 20-minute time moves to the small line under it. Pausing or resetting the main timer stops the hold. Each strength day uses the next of the guide's 3 distributions for that day and avoids the exercises you did last time; that history is saved when you press Start. The lead-in and each break end with three ticks and a high “go” beep. Each exercise ends with three ticks and an “awooga” horn, and the last one gets three horns in a row. Space bar starts and pauses.
